## Edit direction

- Start with a 0.8-second Schoolment logo reveal before the dashboard.
- Use large, high-contrast captions, with one keyword highlighted in violet.
- Add a gentle 105–110% push-in on every screenshot so the Short feels active.
- Use a short whoosh between screenshots; keep the voice clear and ahead of the music.
- Finish with the website and a “Book a free demo” call-to-action for at least two seconds.
