Exam Season Survival Guide: Automating Report Card Generation

A practical guide to getting through exam season without the report card crunch — what to set up in advance, and what to automate.

Published August 14, 2026

The crunch is almost always at the end, not throughout

Marks usually get entered steadily through the exam period — the actual crunch hits afterward, when every mark has to be compiled, checked, and turned into a formatted report card for every student, all at once, under a deadline.

What to set up before exam season starts

  • Exam templates and mark entry structures ready before the first exam, not built under time pressure afterward.
  • Clear ownership of who enters marks for which subject, so nothing is waiting on one person at the end.
  • A report card format finalized in advance — changing the format after marks are already entered creates rework.

Where automation actually helps here

Once marks are recorded, generating the report card itself is mechanical — pulling the right marks into the right format for each student. That's exactly the kind of repetitive task worth automating, so the crunch shrinks from days of manual compilation to a review step.

Schoolment's approach

The Exams & Report Cards module generates report cards directly from recorded marks, and exam creation itself can run on a schedule once your school turns that on — reducing the same manual setup work every term.
