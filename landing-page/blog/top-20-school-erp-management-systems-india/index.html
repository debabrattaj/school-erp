Introduction

India has many good school ERP and school management systems, each with its own strengths. To help schools explore the options, we have shortlisted 20 platforms worth considering in 2026. This is a starting point for comparison, not a claim that one system is the best for every school. The right choice depends on your school's needs, budget and daily workflows.

This guide lists twenty school ERP and school management systems to consider in 2026. It draws on official product information, not hands-on benchmark tests. Selection reflects published coverage of school administration, academics or parent engagement, with a mix of integrated and open-source options.

1. Entab CampusCare

CampusCare is Entab's school ERP offering. Its published coverage includes student records, fees, results, library operations, assignments, circulars, messages and staff salary workflows. This makes it a candidate for schools comparing a broad administrative system with parent-facing communication.

Ask the team to demonstrate your actual report-card format and fee structure, rather than a generic sample. For a school group, request a walkthrough of campus-level access and consolidated reporting. Confirm the exact CampusCare edition, modules and implementation services in the quotation.

Explore Entab CampusCare's official product information.

2. Schoolment

Schoolment brings admissions, fees, attendance, timetables, exams and parent communication into a connected school management system. Its published module suite also includes an LMS, an online exam portal, accounts, payroll, library, hostel and transport workflows. Sai, the Schoolment AI assistant, is positioned around parent support.

Consider it if your school wants to evaluate administration and learning tools together. In a demo, follow one student from admission to fee collection, attendance and report-card publication. Ask which modules, mobile features and AI workflows are included in your proposed deployment, and confirm hosting, migration, backups and support separately.

Explore Schoolment's official product information.

3. Fedena

Fedena publishes features for admissions, attendance, timetables, fee collection, examinations and gradebooks. It also lists parent collaboration, mobile apps and integrations for payments and online meetings. Schools seeking a configurable administrative platform can include it in their evaluation.

Use the demo to test your concessions, optional fee heads, examination rules and promotion process. Ask which features are core, which depend on add-ons, and what configuration your team can change without vendor assistance. Confirm that required integrations are supported in the quoted plan.

Explore Fedena's official product information.

4. MyClassboard

MyClassboard describes a suite spanning admission CRM, student information, finance, HR and payroll, transport and other school operations. Its published learning features include course management, teaching plans, homework and online examinations, alongside parent communication channels.

It is worth considering when several departments need a shared platform. Ask an admissions officer, accountant and academic coordinator to test their own tasks in the same demonstration. Check whether LMS, communications, banking connections and mobile access are bundled or priced separately.

Explore MyClassboard's official product information.

5. Edunext ERP

Edunext offers a cloud-based school ERP with web and mobile access. Its official site describes online fee collection, admissions, learning tools and integrations such as GPS bus tracking and barcode-based library operations. It also lists access-control and audit features.

Schools comparing hardware-linked workflows should ask for a demonstration using the devices and payment arrangements they intend to retain. Confirm migration coverage for previous academic years, the responsibilities of third-party providers, and how the school can review access logs and restore data when needed.

6. Vidyalaya

Vidyalaya lists academic, admissions, staff and attendance management alongside mobile, portal and e-exam capabilities. Its published integration options include biometric systems, payments, Tally, vehicle tracking and WhatsApp. These are useful areas to investigate for schools connecting existing systems.

Prepare an inventory of the tools your school already uses and ask which connections are available for those exact products. Test the flow of information between systems and who handles failed synchronisation. A listed integration should still be verified for compatibility, setup charges and ongoing support.

7. SkoolBeep

SkoolBeep combines a school ERP proposition with classroom and home-learning offerings. Its published administrative features include student information, fees, transport, parent access, report cards and inventory. The wider platform also describes teaching materials, assessments and classroom technology.

Consider it when both school administration and teaching resources are on the shortlist. Ask for separate scope and costs for ERP software, learning content and any classroom hardware. Teachers should review curriculum relevance and assessment workflows independently of the office team's administrative evaluation.

8. School Diary

School Diary presents a mobile-first platform covering parent engagement, fee payments, virtual classes, attendance, child safety and performance reporting. Its communication features include individual and group messages, events and surveys, with access through mobile and web interfaces.

It may suit a shortlist where parent adoption and communication are central priorities. Ask parents and teachers to try common tasks, including finding homework and acknowledging a notice. 9. SchoolPad

SchoolPad describes school management features covering student information, attendance, fees, homework, library records, transport data and HR-related processes. Demonstrate school-specific report cards and parent workflows before committing.

School ERP, school management system or LMS?

These labels often overlap. A school management system generally covers student records and daily administration. A broader ERP connects those records with financial and operational processes. An LMS focuses on teaching materials, assignments, learning activities and assessments.

One supplier may offer all three, but the name alone does not tell you what is included. If your school needs both administration and digital learning, ask to see a student's class allocation flow into courses and assessments without duplicate entry. Our guides to school ERP workflows and LMS capabilities explain the distinction in more detail.

How to choose the right school ERP in India

Shortlist three providers and give each the same school scenarios. Use anonymised sample records instead of sharing real student information during early demonstrations.

  • Admissions: Convert an enquiry into an admitted student, check required documents and show how the record reaches the accounts team.
  • Fees: Apply a sibling concession, accept a partial payment, reconcile an online payment and correct an error with an audit trail.
  • Academics: Build your actual examination pattern and report card. A claim of board support is not a substitute for checking your school's grading rules.
  • Parent access: Find a notice, homework, attendance and fee status on an ordinary mobile phone. Check access for families with multiple children.
  • Data and permissions: Demonstrate staff access restrictions, exports, backups and the process for recovering records. Ask who owns each operational responsibility.
  • Implementation: Agree on migration checks, staff training, the launch timetable and support escalation before signing.

For AI features, ask what school data the assistant can access, how permissions are enforced, when it hands over to a person and how incorrect answers are handled. An AI label is not evidence of dependable automation.

Keep a written scorecard. See our school ERP vendor comparison framework for a structured approach.

Compare total cost, not just the subscription

There is no reliable single price that represents every school's needs. Ask all shortlisted suppliers to quote for the same student count, campuses, modules and contract period. Include implementation, data migration, training, mobile access, communication usage, payment-related charges and any hardware or third-party subscriptions.

Also ask about renewal terms, customisation, storage, support coverage and data export when leaving. For an open-source option, add hosting and the time needed to maintain the system. A lower software licence cost does not automatically mean a lower total operating cost.

Frequently asked questions

Which is the best school management system in India?

There is no universal winner. The best fit is the system that demonstrates your priority workflows, meets your service requirements and is practical for staff and parents to use. Use this list to narrow options, then test them against the same requirements.

Can a small school use a school ERP?

Yes, but start with the work that creates the most friction: student records, fees, attendance and parent updates. Ask whether you can enable additional modules later without re-entering data or changing platforms.

Should schools choose cloud-based software?

Cloud software can make access across office and mobile devices easier. Still, check connectivity requirements, hosting responsibilities, backups, access controls and exit arrangements. Compare these with the resources required to operate an on-premises or self-hosted system.

Does every school ERP include an LMS and online exams?

No. The availability and depth of these tools vary. Request a demonstration of course creation, assignments, test delivery, marking and result publication, then confirm which features are included in the plan.

How can we evaluate Schoolment?

Book a Schoolment demo with your admissions, accounts and academic teams. Bring a sample fee structure and report-card format so the discussion can focus on your daily operations rather than a generic feature tour.

Choose the system your school can use confidently

A useful school ERP connects people and records without making everyday work harder. Whether you choose Schoolment or another provider, prioritise accurate data, clear responsibilities and a successful pilot. Test the routine tasks first: those are the tasks your school will repeat every day.
