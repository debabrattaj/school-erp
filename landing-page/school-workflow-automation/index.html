Scheduled workflows

Take recurring tasks off the office checklist.

These workflows run after the applicable school features, rules and server schedules are configured. They are not all switched on by default.

01

Recurring fee generation

When: A fee structure reaches its scheduled billing date.

Creates fees for applicable students, applies the configured deadline rule and advances the next billing date. Scheduled billing checks the student, fee type, year and billing period to avoid repeat charges.

Less repetitive class-by-class billing.

What needs to be configured?

Enable the school’s fee automation feature and configure fee structures and the server schedule.

02

Fee reminders

When: A configured reminder stage becomes due.

Follows up using the current outstanding amount. Each reminder stage is recorded, and repeat runs do not resend the same stage for the same fee. Older stages can be superseded when a later stage is already due.

Less manual chasing of unpaid balances.

What needs to be configured?

Configure reminder rules, valid guardian contacts and the required messaging provider.

03

Late-fee calculation

When: An unpaid fee passes its deadline and grace period.

Recalculates the applicable late charge from the configured amount, frequency and grace days, then updates the balance and payment status. Re-running for the same date does not simply add the charge again.

Consistent application of the school’s late-fee policy.

What needs to be configured?

Enable late charges and review the school’s policy settings before scheduling the job.

04

Recurring exam creation

When: An active exam template reaches its next run date.

Creates the exam for the applicable academic year and advances the template’s schedule. Missing academic-year setup and naming conflicts require attention instead of being silently treated as successful creation.

Less repeated setup of familiar assessments.

What needs to be configured?

Enable exam automation, define templates and create the relevant academic years.

05

Year-end student processing

When: The configured year-end processing date arrives.

Applies result-based promotion, detention or graduation processing. Students without marks or a confidently identified destination class are left for staff review.

Less repetitive year-end enrolment work.

What needs to be configured?

Enable scheduled promotion and review marks, class mappings and academic-year settings.

06

Admissions follow-up reminders

When: Assigned inquiry follow-ups or admissions tasks become due or overdue.

Sends the assigned staff member an email digest of work requiring attention. Closed inquiries are excluded. This reminds the staff member; it does not automatically call or negotiate with a family.

Fewer forgotten callbacks and pending tasks.

What needs to be configured?

Assign tasks to linked staff accounts and configure email delivery and the scheduled job.

07

Overdue library reminders

When: A borrowed book is overdue by a configured interval.

Runs the configured overdue-reminder stages for eligible student or staff borrowers, recording sent, skipped and superseded outcomes. These reminders concern already overdue loans.

Less time maintaining manual overdue lists.

What needs to be configured?

Enable library reminders, configure rules and ensure borrower contact information is available.

08

Biometric attendance synchronisation

When: A configured device integration sends punches or a scheduled pull runs.

Imports punches through compatible push or pull integrations. A LAN-only device needs a suitable local connector or vendor service; registering a device alone does not make it reachable.

Less re-entry of device attendance records.

What needs to be configured?

Enable biometric attendance, configure a supported integration and map device users correctly.

Action-triggered workflows

Make everyday work easier to finish.

Your team starts the task or confirms the event. Schoolment handles the connected calculations and record preparation.

Payments & receipts

After a verified gateway confirmation or authorised payment entry, Schoolment updates the applicable fee records, calculates balances and payment status, and generates receipt numbers where applicable. A parent-reported transfer still needs the appropriate verification.

Marks & report cards

Recorded marks drive totals, weighted percentages, grades, pass/fail outcomes and class ranks. Staff can generate report-card PDFs from templates using student records. Result publication remains a separate authorised action.

Timetable generation

Staff request a draft using class subjects, teachers and required periods. The generator checks teacher and class clashes and preserves existing breaks. Unplaced periods are reported for review rather than hidden.

Leave & substitute cover

Approving staff leave calculates working leave days, updates balances and raises substitute-cover requirements from the affected timetable. Staff still choose and assign appropriate substitute cover.

Payroll preparation

Staff generate a period’s payslips from configured salary structures. The system calculates the payroll records and skips payslips already generated for that period. It does not transfer salaries to bank accounts.

Operational dashboards

Attendance and fee records feed calculated summaries, so staff can review recorded activity without rebuilding the same totals in a separate spreadsheet. The figures depend on complete, current source records.

People remain in control

Automate the repeatable steps.
Review the important decisions.

Publishing results, approving payment exceptions or refunds, verifying documents and assigning substitute teachers still involve authorised staff. Automation supports those responsibilities; it does not remove them.

Transport integrations also support tracking-related processing: scheduled housekeeping can flag silent devices and age out old location history. This depends on configured tracking and retention jobs.

A practical setup checklist

  1. Choose the workflows your school actually needs.
  2. Enable the relevant school features.
  3. Configure rules, contacts, mappings and dates.
  4. Connect required providers and server schedules.
  5. Review a preview or dry run where available.
  6. Check run logs and assign someone to resolve exceptions.
Common questions

Know what runs—and what needs your team.

Does buying the ERP turn every automation on?

No. Scheduled workflows generally need the relevant school feature enabled, rules configured and a server job scheduled. Message delivery also needs valid contact details and the appropriate provider setup.

Are all these tasks powered by AI?

No. Most workflows described here use configured rules, dates and calculations. They do not need a generative AI model to decide which fee is due or how a total is calculated.

Can staff review the results?

Yes. Scheduled billing and reminder workflows retain run or delivery outcomes, and staff review operational records in their modules. Missing data and unsupported cases may still require manual attention.

Will a repeated fee job charge the student again?

Scheduled billing checks for an existing charge for the student, fee type, academic year and billing period. Configure the fee structure correctly and review any older manual entries whose billing period is missing or ambiguous.

Where should a school start?

Start with one repetitive workflow, such as recurring fee generation, and verify its records. Add reminders once due dates and guardian contacts are reliable. A demo can help identify the setup needed for your school.
