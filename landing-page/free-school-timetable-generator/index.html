Free School Timetable Generator

Arrange your school week with teacher-clash checks, fixed lessons, double periods and printable class or teacher timetables.

Up to 5 sectionsNo sign-upRuns in your browser

How to fill your school week

Weekly capacity = working days × teaching periods per day. For example, 5 days × 6 periods gives 30 periods for each section. Configure up to two breaks using the period after which each starts and its duration. Set its after-period value to 0 to turn a break off. Both breaks appear as labelled rows or columns with their times. Breaks do not count as teaching periods, so 6 daily teaching periods still means 30 per week.

  1. Set your working days and daily periods.
  2. Add one teaching assignment for each subject in each section. An assignment means, for example, “Anita teaches Maths to 6-A for 6 periods per week”.
  3. Add up the Periods per week values separately for each section. To fill a 30-period week, that section's subject totals must equal 30. A smaller total leaves free periods; a larger total will not fit.
  4. Use the same teacher name consistently across sections so the generator can prevent clashes. Keep consecutive periods at 1 for regular lessons. For a two-period lab, set weekly periods to 2 and consecutive periods to 2; it occupies two slots, not four.
  5. Click Generate timetable, review any unplaced lessons, then select a section or teacher view and your preferred row/column layout before printing.

Try a sample school loads 6-A and 6-B with 8 subjects each (16 assignment rows). Each section has Maths 6 + English 5 + Science 5 + Social Studies 4 + Second Language 5 + Computer Lab 2 + Physical Education 2 + Art 1 = 30 periods. Click Generate timetable to fill the complete week. You can adjust this example to your school's subjects.

01 / Set up your school week

Build up to five sections together so shared teachers and rooms are checked across the whole week.

02 / Add teaching assignments

Use consistent section, teacher and room names. Enter fixed lesson starts and unavailable periods as Mon:1, Tue:3. Teacher unavailability applies to all their assignments. A double lesson uses two weekly periods and cannot cross either break.

03 / Review your timetable

Move a lesson

Moves are checked against the same clash and availability rules. Fixed lessons must be changed in setup.

A useful first draft, with constraints you control

Empty slots stay free. If the bounded search cannot place every lesson, the result is labelled partial and lists the missing lessons. This does not prove a schedule is impossible. Adjust loads, fixed lessons or availability and try again. Review workload balance before sharing.

Timetable processing stays in your browser. Save draft stores setup on this device only. Download CSV opens in Excel or other spreadsheets. For PDF, choose Save as PDF in your browser's print dialog; landscape works well for wider tables.
