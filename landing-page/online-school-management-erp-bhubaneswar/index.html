School management software FAQs for Bhubaneswar

What should a Bhubaneswar school look for in school management software?

Look for software that connects the workflows your team uses every day, offers clear role-based access, works on mobile devices and can reflect your fee and assessment structure. A useful demonstration should follow one real process from start to finish rather than showing isolated screens.

Can Schoolment support CBSE, ICSE and Odisha state-board schools?

Yes. Subjects, academic years, assessment structures, fee heads and report-card layouts can be configured for the school instead of being fixed to one board.

Can we move existing student data from spreadsheets?

Schoolment provides bulk-import tools for student and operational records. During planning, the team can review your current files, decide what should be migrated and clean the data before import.

Do parents and teachers get mobile access?

Yes. Schoolment includes web access plus Android and iOS apps. Permissions determine what each staff or parent account can view and manage.

Can we start with only fees, attendance or admissions?

Yes. A school can prioritise the modules it needs first and bring other workflows into the same platform as its rollout progresses.
