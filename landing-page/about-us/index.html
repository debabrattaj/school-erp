What we do

Schoolment is a school management platform covering admissions, fee collection and online payments, attendance and timetabling, exams and report cards, a parent & teacher portal, and Sai — an AI chatbot that answers parents' routine questions instantly instead of routing them to office staff.

Every module is built to reduce manual, repeated work: fee billing, year-end promotion, and exam creation can all run on a schedule once a school turns them on, instead of someone re-doing the same steps every term.

How we build

  • Each school's data lives in its own isolated database — never mixed or pooled with another school's.
  • Scheduled automation is opt-in, not on by default — a school administrator has to explicitly switch on each fee, promotion, or exam schedule.
  • Every automated run is logged, so an admin can see exactly what happened and when, without SSHing into a server.
  • Free data migration, updates and support are included for as long as a school is with us — not sold as add-ons.

Where we are

Schoolment is based in Bhubaneswar, Odisha, India, and works with schools directly on onboarding, migration and support rather than through resellers.
