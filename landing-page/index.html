Beyond the module list

It bends to your school, not the other way round

Every school runs differently. These are the things you can change yourself, on day one, without a support ticket.

Roles you define yourself

Build your own roles — an Academic Coordinator who can manage exams but only view fees, an accountant who never sees counselling notes. Every module gets its own no access / view / manage setting, and the same rules drive the web app and the mobile app.

Two-factor authentication

Staff accounts can be locked behind an authenticator app, so a leaked password alone is not enough to open your student database.

Dashboards you arrange

Drag charts into the layout your principal actually reads — fees collected by type, students by class or house, grade distribution, outstanding dues — as trends, donuts or tables.

Bulk import, not retyping

Students, teachers, books, vehicles, hostel rooms and inventory all import from a spreadsheet, so migrating year-old records is an afternoon, not a term.

Forms and layouts that match your school

Reorder and rename the fields on a student record, add your own, and set which ones your office must fill in — without waiting on a release from us.

Android and iOS app included

The same 40+ modules, the same permissions, on a phone. Staff mark attendance, enter marks and grade handed-in homework from the classroom; parents get fees, marks, attendance, study material and homework — handed in as a photo of the work — without a desktop.

Multi-language, including right-to-left

The interface ships in English, French and Arabic, and lays itself out right-to-left when the language calls for it.

Multi-curriculum, multi-school

Run CBSE, ICSE, state board or an international track side by side with their own subject groups and assessment schemes — and run more than one school from a single platform console.

Runs itself

Set it once. It runs on a schedule.

Eight things your office used to do by hand, every single cycle — now they fire themselves on dates you choose. Each one is opt-in and logged: nothing runs unattended until you switch it on.

Automatic fee billing

Fee structures bill themselves monthly, quarterly or annually on the date you set — no one has to run "Bulk Class" billing by hand, ever again.

Fee reminders that chase for you

Reminder rules you define fire on their own over WhatsApp and email, so overdue fees get followed up without anyone keeping a list.

Late fees, applied on time

Overdue fees pick up the late charge your policy specifies on the day it is due — consistently, and without an argument about who forgot.

Self-building exam calendar

Recurring exams — Unit Tests, Half-Yearlies, Annuals — create themselves each academic year on schedule. Or copy a past year's calendar in one click.

Timetables that draw themselves

Generate a clash-free timetable for every class at once, instead of moving periods around a whiteboard until the conflicts stop.

Library reminders

Books coming due and books already overdue prompt their borrowers on a schedule, so the librarian is not the reminder system.

Admission follow-ups

Enquiries that have gone quiet get chased automatically, so a family who filled in a form in April is not lost by June.

Automatic year-end promotion

On the date you choose, every student is promoted, detained or graduated automatically — using the same result-based logic your office reviews by hand today.
