Online School Management ERP for Schools in Kolkata

Schoolment is a cloud-based school ERP for admissions, fees, attendance, exams and parent communication — built in Bhubaneswar and set up remotely for schools in Kolkata, West Bengal, with the same free migration and onboarding as any other school.

Book a free demo ← Back to home

Why Kolkata schools pick Schoolment

  • Admissions, fees, attendance, exams, and parent communication in one portal — no more juggling separate registers and spreadsheets.
  • Each school gets its own isolated database — never mixed or pooled with another school's data.
  • Scheduled automation for fee billing, year-end promotion, and exam creation — opt-in, logged, and off by default until you switch it on.
  • Sai, an AI chatbot, answers parents' routine questions instantly, any hour — without your front office fielding every call.
  • WhatsApp integration for announcements and fee reminders, since that's where parents already are.

Set up without a site visit

Because Schoolment runs entirely in the browser, a school in Kolkata gets the exact same onboarding as one down the street from our Bhubaneswar base: a video walkthrough, remote data migration, and ongoing support by call, email and WhatsApp. No installation, no local server, nothing to visit an office for.

Works with any curriculum

Whether a Kolkata school follows CBSE, ICSE, or the West Bengal State Board syllabus, Schoolment's fee structures, exam templates, and report cards are configured per school — not locked to one board's format.
