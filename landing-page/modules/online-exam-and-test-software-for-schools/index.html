Everything Online Exam covers

Multiple choice & true/false

Build a quiz question by question from these two auto-gradable formats.

Instant grading

No manual marking — every submission is scored the second it's submitted.

⏱️ Optional timer

Set a time limit; students see a live countdown, and the test auto-submits the moment time runs out.

Login required, one attempt

Students take a test through their own portal login, not an open link anyone could find.

Full answer review

After submitting, students see exactly which answers were right and wrong, with the correct answer shown.

Results, already tallied

See every student's score for a test the moment they submit — nothing to add up by hand.

Paid add-on — sold separately

Add browser-lockdown proctoring to any test

Switch this on for a school and a teacher can require it per test. It asks a student's browser to go fullscreen and reports tab switches, window switches and copy/paste attempts as flags for a teacher to review — never as an automatic verdict. A school can also opt a policy into periodic webcam snapshots on top of the browser signals below.

Fullscreen required

A proctored attempt asks the browser to go fullscreen the moment it starts, and logs the exact moment a student leaves it.

Optional webcam snapshots

A policy can turn on periodic still photos during an attempt — a snapshot every so many seconds, not a continuous recording — reviewable by a teacher alongside the browser signals above.

On-device face check

With webcam snapshots on, a small AI model running entirely in the student's browser flags when no face — or more than one — is visible. No video ever leaves the browser for this, no identity is recognised, just a flag for a teacher to look at.

Tab & window switching flagged

Switching to another tab or clicking away to another window during the attempt is reported the instant it happens.

Copy & paste blocked

Copying a question or pasting into an answer is blocked in the browser and logged as a flag, not just quietly allowed.

Guardian consent, every time

A parent or the school office grants consent per student before a proctored test can even be started — a student can never grant this for themselves, and the same consent covers webcam snapshots when a policy uses them.

Violation-based auto-submit

Cross a school-set number of flags and the attempt submits automatically on what was already saved — the same safeguard already built into the timer.

A prompt for a teacher, not a verdict

Every flag lands in a timeline a teacher reviews by hand — a browser signal is evidence to look at, never proof the system acts on by itself.
