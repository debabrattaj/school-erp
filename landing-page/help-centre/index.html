Frequently asked

Common questions

How do I log in to Schoolment?

Your school gives you a private portal link once your account is set up — we don't publish it here. Ask your school administrator, or contact us if you can't find yours. If your school hasn't been set up on Schoolment yet, book a demo first.

I forgot my password — how do I reset it?

Use "Forgot password" on your school's portal sign-in screen, or ask your school's Admin to reset it for you from Users & Access.

How does automatic fee, promotion, and exam generation work?

Each one is opt-in twice: your school's platform owner has to enable it for the school first, and then your Admin switches it on for the specific fee structure, academic year, or exam template. Nothing runs automatically until both are on, and every scheduled run is logged so you can see exactly what happened.

Can I try Schoolment before committing?

Yes — book a 30-minute demo with your own school's context. There's no commitment required, and setup, data migration, and support are included at no extra cost.

How is my school's data kept secure?

Every school gets an isolated database, passwords are hashed (never stored in plain text), and all traffic runs over HTTPS. See Privacy & Security for the full picture.

How do I contact support?

Email info@schoolment.com and we'll get back to you within 1 business day.

How do I keep school data secured?

Security here has two layers: what Schoolment does, and what your school controls. Schoolment isolates each school in its own database, hashes every password, and encrypts all traffic over HTTPS. Your school controls day-to-day security through Users & Access — grant each staff member only the role they need (Admin, Teacher, Accountant, and so on), and remove access as soon as someone leaves. See Privacy & Security for the full picture.

How do I browse a previous year's data?

Nothing gets deleted at year-end — promoting students creates a new enrollment for the new academic year while last year's stays exactly as it was. Switch the academic year filter on screens like Student Enrollments, Fees, Exams, and Report Cards to look back at any previous year's records.

How does AI provide 24/7 support to parents?

Sai, Schoolment's AI chatbot, lives inside the parent portal and answers fee, attendance, marks, and timetable questions in plain language, straight from your school's live records — any time of day, with no office hours to wait on. A guardian's Sai only ever answers about their own linked children.

How can an LMS or school ERP accelerate a school's growth?

Mostly by giving your office time back: fee billing, year-end promotion, and exam creation can run on a schedule instead of someone redoing the same steps every term, and Sai plus WhatsApp answer routine parent questions without a call to the front desk. On the admissions side, the CRM's pipeline and public apply-online form mean fewer enquiries slip through the cracks — which is usually where growth is lost, not where it's gained.

How does the school admission CRM work?

Every enquiry — however it comes in — lands in one visual pipeline with stages like Inquiry, Contacted, and Enrolled, plus a full follow-up history. Parents can also apply directly through a public, school-branded link you share, with no account needed, and they get an automatic confirmation email the moment they submit. When you're ready, converting an enquiry to a student is one click, and the admission number is generated automatically.

How do I proctor online exams?

Schoolment's online exam module doesn't include webcam or AI proctoring — its integrity controls are login-based access (students take a test through their own portal account, not an open link anyone could find), one attempt per test, and an optional timer that auto-submits the moment time runs out. Physical invigilation for high-stakes exams is still something your school handles directly.
